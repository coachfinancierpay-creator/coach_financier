cascade - DECISION TREE — CAR INSURANCE
==================================

Role: choose and justify a car insurance plan suited to the vehicle and to the customer's profile.
Usage: follow the steps in order; apply the first condition that matches the customer.


STEP 1 — Scope
- IF the customer is NOT looking to insure a vehicle: THEN no car insurance recommendation (leave the flow).
- OTHERWISE: go to step 2.


STEP 2 — Is the vehicle under LOA / LLD / leasing?
- IF YES: recommend the « Leasing » plan (priority).
- IF NO: go to step 3.


STEP 3 — Base plan according to the vehicle's value and age
- New / recent / high-value vehicle          → « Comprehensive+ »
- Recent vehicle / medium to high value      → « Comprehensive »
- Older vehicle / moderate value             → « Third Party+ »
- Very old / low-value vehicle               → « Third Party »


STEP 4 — Adjustment according to customer risk
Take these factors into account (they may raise the plan level or adjust the deductibles):
- young driver;
- recent claims;
- bonus / malus;
- annual mileage;
- parking;
- private or professional use;
- geographical area.


STEP 5 — Adjustment according to protection needs
Check the guarantees / options useful to the customer:
- theft;
- vandalism;
- glass breakage;
- all-accident damage (damage to the customer's own vehicle);
- replacement vehicle;
- 0 km assistance;
- enhanced driver protection.


STEP 6 — Potential discounts to check
- low-mileage driver;
- electric vehicle;
- eligible young driver;
- good bonus;
- child of an SG policyholder.


STEP 7 — Estimate
- Calculate the cost and the level of protection obtained.


STEP 8 — Answer to the customer (LLM)
Explain in English, clearly and pedagogically:
- the recommendation retained and why;
- a cheaper alternative;
- a more protective alternative.
