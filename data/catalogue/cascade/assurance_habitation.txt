cascade - DECISION TREE — HOME INSURANCE
========================================

Role: determine whether home insurance is required and which plan to recommend according to the occupancy situation.
Usage: follow the steps in order; apply the first condition that matches the customer.


STEP 1 — Scope
- IF the customer does NOT occupy a dwelling: THEN no home insurance to propose (exit).
- OTHERWISE: go to step 2.


STEP 2 — Customer's occupancy status
- Tenant:
  THEN home insurance is MANDATORY. Choose the level:
    - tight budget               → « Initial » plan
    - standard protection        → « Comfort » plan
    - enhanced protection        → « Optimal » plan
- Owner-occupier:
  THEN home insurance is strongly recommended. Choose:
    - simple dwelling / small budget → « Initial »
    - standard use                   → « Comfort »
    - high-value dwelling / strong protection → « Optimal »
- Student:
  THEN propose the « Student plan ».
- Landlord owner / unoccupied dwelling:
  THEN propose the « Non-occupant owner plan ».


STEP 3 — Adjustment according to the dwelling's risks
Take into account: value of the furniture; valuables; location; house or flat; floor; outbuildings; claims history; theft risk; equipment.


STEP 4 — Useful options and guarantees
- Add the relevant options / guarantees according to the profile and the dwelling.


STEP 5 — Answer to the customer (LLM)
Explain in English the recommendation retained and why.
