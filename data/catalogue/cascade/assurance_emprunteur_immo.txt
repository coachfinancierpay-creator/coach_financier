cascade - DECISION TREE — MORTGAGE PROTECTION INSURANCE
===================================================

Role: determine whether mortgage protection insurance is needed and which cover / insured share to recommend.
Usage: follow the steps in order; apply the first condition that matches the customer.


STEP 1 — Scope
- IF the customer has NO mortgage in progress and NO mortgage project: THEN no mortgage protection insurance (exit).
- OTHERWISE: go to step 2.


STEP 2 — Use of the property
- Primary or secondary residence:
  THEN propose comprehensive cover: death + PTIA + incapacity + disability.
- Rental investment:
  THEN propose cover suited to an investor (death / PTIA at minimum).
- Senior profile:
  THEN propose an adapted senior plan.


STEP 3 — Public sector / BFM customer?
- IF YES: check and compare with BFM mortgage protection insurance.
- IF NO: go to step 4.


STEP 4 — Number of borrowers
- 1 borrower: insured share generally 100 %.
- 2 borrowers: have the insured share chosen (e.g. 50/50, 70/30, 100/100, etc.).


STEP 5 — Professional and family situation
Take into account: employee; self-employed; high-risk occupations; spouse's income; dependent children.


STEP 6 — Loss of employment option
- Assess whether the customer needs the « loss of employment » option.


STEP 7 — Contract comparison
- Compare the SG mortgage protection insurance contract with external delegation.


STEP 8 — Cost
- Calculate the total cost of the insurance.


STEP 9 — Answer to the customer (LLM)
Explain in English the recommended cover (guarantees + insured share) and the comparison elements.
