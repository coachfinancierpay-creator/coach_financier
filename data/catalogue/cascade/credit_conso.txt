cascade - DECISION TREE — CONSUMER LOAN
================================

Role: recommend the consumer loan (or cash payment) best suited to the customer's need and profile.
Usage: follow the steps in order; apply the first condition that matches the customer.


STEP 1 — Scope
- IF the request does NOT concern a consumer financing need: THEN no consumer loan recommendation (exit).
- OTHERWISE: go to step 2.


STEP 2 — Can the customer finance without a loan?
- Can the customer pay for the project in cash while keeping sufficient emergency savings?
- IF YES: recommend a cash payment (or partly cash), then possibly compare with a loan.
- IF NO: go to step 3.


STEP 3 — Loan consolidation?
- IF YES:
    - BFM-eligible customer → compare « BFM Perspective » vs « Compact Loan ».
    - Otherwise → « Compact Loan ».
- IF NO: go to step 4.


STEP 4 — Vehicle project?
- IF YES:
    - BFM-eligible customer → compare « BFM Freedom Loan Auto » vs « Expresso Auto Loan ».
    - Otherwise → « Expresso Auto Loan ».
- IF NO: go to step 5.


STEP 5 — Student customer?
- IF YES:
    - Public-sector school student → « BFM Student Loan ».
    - Driving licence + age 15-25 → « €1-a-day Driving Licence Loan ».
    - Otherwise → « Progressive Student Loan ».
- IF NO: go to step 6.


STEP 6 — Young professional (18-29)?
- IF YES:
    - BFM eligible → compare « BFM Young Professionals Loan » / « BFM Freedom Loan ».
    - Otherwise → « Expresso Young Professional Loan ».
- IF NO: go to step 7.


STEP 7 — BFM eligible / public sector?
- IF YES:
    - Mobility / career change → « BFM Mobility / Career Change Loan ».
    - Exact need of 1 500 € + eligible profile → « BFM Advance Loan ».
    - Standard personal project → « BFM Freedom Loan ».
- IF NO: go to step 8.


STEP 8 — One-off or recurring need?
- ONE-OFF need → « Expresso Loan ».
- RECURRING need / cash reserve → « Alterna Revolving Credit ».


STEP 9 — Final affordability check
Check: monthly payment; remaining income after charges; debt-to-income ratio; banking incidents; remaining savings; total cost of the loan.


STEP 10 — Answer to the customer (AI)
Explain in English: the recommendation retained and why; alternatives; points of vigilance.
