cascade - DECISION TREE — SAVINGS / INVESTMENT
=======================================

Role: recommend the savings or investment product best suited to the customer's situation and goal.
Usage: follow the steps in order; apply the first condition that matches the customer.


STEP 1 — Sufficient emergency savings?
- IF NO (the customer has no emergency savings):
    - Customer eligible for the LEP?
        - YES → propose the « LEP » as a priority.
        - NO → « Livret A savings account » available?
            - YES → propose the « Livret A savings account ».
            - NO → propose the « LDDS ».
- IF YES (sufficient emergency savings): go to step 2.


STEP 2 — Customer's goal
- Short-term project (< 3 years):
    - Need for immediate liquidity? YES → « Livret A savings account » / « LDDS » / « CSL ».
    - Need for immediate liquidity? NO → « Term Deposit Account ».
- Property project:
    - Need for flexible liquidity → « CEL ».
    - Medium-term horizon → « PEL ».
- Retirement:
    - Customer with sufficient taxable income? YES → « PER » as a priority.
    - Customer with sufficient taxable income? NO → « Life Insurance » / « PEA » depending on risk.
- Long-term wealth building:
    - Low risk tolerance   → « Life Insurance » mostly in the euro fund.
    - Medium risk tolerance  → multi-support « Life Insurance ».
    - High risk tolerance   → « PEA » / « PEA-PME ».
- Estate planning / inheritance:
    → « Life Insurance ».
- Money available without a specific goal:
    → Assess the horizon and risk tolerance, then guide towards: savings accounts / « CAT » / « Life Insurance » / « PEA ».
